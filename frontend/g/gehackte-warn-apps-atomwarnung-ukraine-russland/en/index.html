Rumour from 2026-11-01

Nuclear and radiation alarm via hacked warning apps

What is being claimed

The official warning apps in Ukraine and in Russia showed messages about nuclear and chemical threats and allegedly launched nuclear warheads.

What fact-checkers say

The Ukrainian Air Force stated there was no radiation danger and the app had apparently been manipulated from outside; Russia’s Ministry of Emergency Situations spoke of a massive cyber attack and false information. According to Al Jazeera, the confusion in Kyiv was cleared up in less than an hour.

What happened in the end

The report does not mention any actual release of radiation; the messages were caused by hacked warning apps on 1 and 2 November.
