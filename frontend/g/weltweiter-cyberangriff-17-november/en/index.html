Rumour from 2026-11-17

Worldwide cyber attack on 17 November 2026

What is being claimed

Social networks claimed that on 17 November 2026 an AI-driven cyber attack on the internet or the power grid would happen, bank accounts would be reset and the internet would be shut down for three months.

What fact-checkers say

IFLScience and Mimikama found no evidence for the claims; the real basis is considered to be an announced nationwide US test of the emergency alert system by FEMA and the FCC. FEMA stated it was a routine test required by law, with no hidden emergency.

What happened in the end

The test ran on 17 November at around 2:20 pm Eastern Time via radio and television, lasted about 18 seconds and triggered no mobile phone alerts. No major technical problems were reported, FEMA called the test successfully completed and rejected claims of a hack as false; Mimikama later reported that the announced blackout did not happen.
