Rumour from 2026-11-17

Worldwide power outage on 17 November 2026

What is being claimed

Social networks claimed that on 17 November 2026 millions of people worldwide would be left without power and communication.

What fact-checkers say

FEMA and the FCC denied having warned of a global power outage; only a nationwide test of the emergency alert system via radio and television was planned. The report names a Simpsons episode showing a power outage on a 17 November as the origin of the date.

What happened in the end

The alert test took place as announced, without any major disruptions reported; a power outage on the claimed scale was not reported in the sources reviewed.
